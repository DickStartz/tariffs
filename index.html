An Example for Understanding Tariffs

You will see two boxes below where you can enter values for the elasticity of imports with respect to the tariff rate (enter a positive number) and your choice of the tariff (where 10 means a 10 percent tariff). The elasticity number is not a policy choice since it depends on the decision of consumers and producers. The tariff rate is a policy choice.
